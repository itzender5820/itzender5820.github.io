01 / a small prayer for you

My dear

Trishu,
you are
my light.

On the first night of Navratri, I just wanted to pause and say this: life feels softer, brighter and a little more magical because you are in it.

You have the heart of a goddess and the laugh that can turn even an ordinary moment into a celebration. I hope you always see yourself the way I see you — powerful, gentle, radiant and completely, wonderfully you.

with all my heart— yours, always
